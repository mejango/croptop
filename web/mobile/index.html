First time here?

Bring your site along.

On your Mac, choose Croptop → Check for Updates… and install version 0.13.20 or later. You can also download Croptop for Mac ↗.

Open your site and choose Connect phone (the phone button beside Settings). Review the hosting permissions and scan its QR code with your phone.

Confirm the connection on both devices, then allow phone posting here. After setup, you can post even when your Mac is asleep.

Have a site key? Import it

Choose your Croptop PRIVATE KEY file. Your key stays on this phone and controls your whole site. Keep your original backup.

Your site

Allow this service to prepare and publish the posts you approve on this phone. Your site key stays here.

Removing this connection deletes the key from this browser. It does not revoke other copies of the key.

Keep Croptop on your home screen

On iPhone, open Safari’s Share menu, then choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, open Chrome’s menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
